See [`../ARCHITECTURE.md`](../ARCHITECTURE.md) for the full design. (The K0/K1, U0/U1, and R1 build plans currently live in the UCC repo under `file-processor-agent/docs/`.)

**Convention:** ADRs are immutable once Accepted. To change a decision, add a new ADR that supersedes it. The first **eight** are the kernel's founding set, accepted as design decisions before the repo is created; they moved into `agent-kernel/docs/adr/` at K0. **0009** and **0010** were added at **R1** (2026-06-05): 0009 when the synchronous orchestrator shipped (the first post-founding ADR, superseding a facet of 0002), and 0010 when the Spring wiring companion landed for the CxO consumer. **0011** and **0012** were added at **R1.3** (2026-06-06): 0011 when the Gemini provider proved the model seam generalizes, and 0012 when the streaming orchestrator landed and surfaced token-streaming as a deferred `1.0` reshape candidate. **0013** was added at **R1.4** (pgvector `Retriever`), and **0014** at **R1.5** (the ring-1 reshape review that confirmed the API freeze-ready and superseded the "ordered enum" facet of 0004). **0015** is the first post-`1.0` ADR (2026-06-06): the additive `1.1` batch closing the human-in-the-loop return path, driven by CVVE — the first real `HumanHandoff` consumer — feeding back friction from its six build slices.

**Consolidation note (2026-06-04):** an initial draft had ten ADRs. Two were merged as facets of larger decisions rather than independent choices: *transport-free tools / defer MCP* folded into **0003** (it's a property of the `Tool` contract), and *defer the synchronous orchestrator to R1* folded into **0002** (it's the rule-of-three applied). The Java-floor and audit ADRs were renumbered to 0007 and 0008.
